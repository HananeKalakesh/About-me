A-03 · Section drawing

How I build a program

The same six stages run through every program I manage, from first concept note to final donor report.

STAGE 1

Assess & design

Needs assessments, program design, and adapting each model to its country and culture.

e.g. YPM: 10 sessions over 3 months
STAGE 2

Fund

Concept notes, proposals, budgets, logframes and work plans.

e.g. YEED with Education Above All
STAGE 3

Partner

MoUs and working relationships with universities, foundations and NGOs.

e.g. University of Monastir
STAGE 4

Train trainers

Training of Trainers, coaching, and onboarding mentors on the tools.

e.g. 15 mentors trained, Aug 2025
STAGE 5

Deliver

Outreach, sessions, workshops and events, run with national teams.

e.g. Career in Tourism workshop
STAGE 6

Measure & report

Pre and post tests, monitoring, impact assessment and donor reports.

e.g. +22% mentor knowledge
